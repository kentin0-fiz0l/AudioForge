AudioForge @VERSION@

Free, open-source audio plugins (MIT License): @VST3@ VST3 plugins, @AU@ Audio Units and @APPS@ standalone apps. Universal: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later.

Click Customize to leave out the VST3 plugins, the Audio Units or the standalone apps.

After installing, restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

AudioForge is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, which is why macOS asked you to confirm before opening this installer. The source code is at github.com/kentin0-fiz0l/AudioForge.
