# SimpleComp User Manual

**AudioForge SimpleComp v1.0.0**

Compressor with threshold, ratio, attack, release, and auto makeup gain

---

## Overview

**SimpleComp** provides transparent and musical compression with:

- **Threshold**: -60 dB to 0 dB (where compression starts)
- **Ratio**: 1:1 to 20:1 (how much to compress)
- **Attack**: 0.1 ms to 100 ms (how fast)
- **Release**: 10 ms to 1000 ms (how fast to recover)
- **Auto Makeup Gain**: Compensates for volume reduction
- **Gain Reduction Meter**: Visual feedback

---

## Quick Start

### Vocal Compression (Natural)

1. **Threshold**: -20 dB
2. **Ratio**: 3:1
3. **Attack**: 10 ms
4. **Release**: 100 ms
5. **Auto Makeup**: On
6. Result: Smooth, controlled vocals

### Drum Bus Glue

1. **Threshold**: -15 dB
2. **Ratio**: 4:1
3. **Attack**: 30 ms
4. **Release**: 150 ms

## Parameters

### Threshold

**Range**: -60 dB to 0 dB
**Description**: Level above which compression kicks in

**Settings**:
- **-30 to -20 dB**: Gentle compression (vocals, mix bus)
- **-20 to -10 dB**: Moderate compression (bass, guitars)
- **-10 to 0 dB**: Heavy compression (limiting, parallel)

---

### Ratio

**Range**: 1:1 to 20:1
**Description**: Amount of gain reduction

**Ratios**:
- **2:1 to 3:1**: Gentle (vocals, mastering)
- **4:1 to 6:1**: Moderate (bass, drums)
- **8:1 to 20:1**: Heavy (limiting, special FX)

---

### Attack

**Range**: 0.1 ms to 100 ms
**Description**: How fast compressor responds

**Settings**:
- **Fast (0.1-5 ms)**: Catches transients (drums, plucks)
- **Medium (10-30 ms)**: Balanced (vocals, guitars)
- **Slow (50-100 ms)**: Lets transients through (keeps punch)

---

### Release

**Range**: 10 ms to 1000 ms
**Description**: How fast gain returns to normal

**Settings**:
- **Fast (10-50 ms)**: Responsive (vocals, fast material)
- **Medium (100-300 ms)**: Musical (most uses)
- **Slow (500-1000 ms)**: Smooth (mastering, pads)

## Use Cases

### 1. Vocal Leveling

**Settings**:
- Threshold: -18 dB
- Ratio: 3:1
- Attack: 5 ms
- Release: 80 ms

**Result**: Even vocal levels

---

### 2. Bass Control

**Settings**:
- Threshold: -15 dB
- Ratio: 5:1
- Attack: 1 ms
- Release: 100 ms

**Result**: Tight, controlled bass

---

### 3. Parallel Compression

**Settings**:
- Threshold: -25 dB
- Ratio: 8:1
- Attack: 0.1 ms
- Release: 50 ms
- Mix: 30-40% parallel

**Result**: Punch + dynamics

---

## Tips

### 1. Watch Gain Reduction Meter

Aim for 3-6 dB GR for natural compression, 8-12 dB for aggressive.

### 2. Use Auto Makeup Gain

Keeps loudness consistent while adjusting settings.

### 3. Slower Attack = More Punch

Let transients through for punchier drums/bass.
