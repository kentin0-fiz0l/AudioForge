# ChorusFlanger User Manual

**AudioForge ChorusFlanger v1.0.0**

LFO-based modulation effects (chorus and flanger)

---

## Overview

**ChorusFlanger** provides time-based modulation with:

- **2 Effect Modes**: Chorus, Flanger
- **Rate**: LFO speed (0.1 - 10 Hz)
- **Depth**: Modulation intensity (0-100%)
- **Feedback**: Regeneration for flanger (0-95%)
- **Mix**: Wet/dry blend

---

## Quick Start

### Lush Chorus

1. **Mode**: Chorus
2. **Rate**: 0.5 Hz (slow)
3. **Depth**: 50%
4. **Mix**: 30%
5. Result: Wide, shimmering chorus

### Jet Flanger

1. **Mode**: Flanger
2. **Rate**: 0.3 Hz
3. **Depth**: 70%
4. **Feedback**: 60%
5. **Mix**: 50%
6. Result: Classic jet/swoosh flanger

---

## Parameters

### Mode

**Chorus**: Wider delay range (10-30ms), thicker sound
**Flanger**: Shorter delay range (0.1-10ms), metallic sound

---

### Rate (LFO Speed)

**Range**: 0.1 Hz - 10 Hz
**Description**: How fast the LFO modulates

- **Slow (0.1-1 Hz)**: Subtle, evolving modulation
- **Medium (1-3 Hz)**: Classic chorus/flanger
- **Fast (5-10 Hz)**: Vibrato-like, intense

---

### Depth

**Range**: 0-100%
**Description**: Intensity of modulation

- **Low (20-40%)**: Subtle movement
- **Medium (50-70%)**: Classic effect
- **High (80-100%)**: Extreme, obvious

---

### Feedback

**Range**: 0-95%
**Description**: Regeneration (flanger only)

- **0%**: Clean, simple flanger
- **40-60%**: Classic jet flanger
- **80-95%**: Extreme, metallic resonance

---

## Use Cases

### 1. Guitar Chorus

**Settings**: Chorus mode, Rate 0.8 Hz, Depth 40%, Mix 25%
**Result**: Classic 80s chorus guitar

### 2. Synth Widening

**Settings**: Chorus mode, Rate 1.5 Hz, Depth 60%, Mix 40%
**Result**: Wide, lush synth pad

### 3. Vocal Doubling

**Settings**: Chorus mode, Rate 0.3 Hz, Depth 30%, Mix 20%
**Result**: Subtle vocal thickening

### 4. Tape Flanger

**Settings**: Flanger mode, Rate 0.2 Hz, Depth 80%, Feedback 70%, Mix 50%
**Result**: Vintage tape flanger effect

---

## Tips

- **Chorus**: Use for widening and thickening (guitars, synths, vocals)
- **Flanger**: Use for dramatic sweeps (transitions, special FX)
- **Low Rate + High Depth**: Slow, evolving modulation
- **High Rate + Low Depth**: Fast vibrato
