## Script

### INTRO (0:00-0:30)

**NARRATION**:
> "Compression is one of the most misunderstood tools in mixing. Today, I'm breaking down SimpleComp and showing you exactly how to use compression to make your vocals sit better, your drums punch harder, and your mix sound professional. Let's dive in!"

---

### PART 1: WHAT IS COMPRESSION? (0:30-1:30)

**[VISUAL: Waveform showing loud and quiet parts]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Compression reduces the dynamic range of audio. See these loud peaks and quiet valleys? A compressor brings them closer together by turning down the loud parts."

**[VISUAL: Show compressor processing, peaks getting reduced]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Why? Because consistent levels sound more polished and professional. Vocals sit better in the mix, drums have more punch, and everything sounds tighter. Let's see how SimpleComp does this."

---

### PART 2: UI & PARAMETERS (1:30-3:00)

**[VISUAL: SimpleComp UI, full screen]**

**NARRATION**:
> "SimpleComp has four main controls. Threshold sets where compression starts - any signal above this level gets compressed."

**[VISUAL: Adjust threshold, show gain reduction meter responding]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Ratio controls how much compression happens. 4:1 means for every 4 dB above threshold, we only let 1 dB through."

**[VISUAL: Change ratio from 2:1 to 8:1, show difference]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Attack controls how fast the compressor reacts. Fast attack catches transients, slow attack lets them through."

**[VISUAL: Demonstrate on drum hit - fast vs slow attack]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Release is how quickly it stops compressing. Fast release sounds responsive, slow release sounds smooth."

**[VISUAL: Show release on vocal phrase]**

**NARRATION**:
> "And Auto Makeup Gain compensates for the volume reduction, so you're hearing compression, not just quieter audio."

---

### PART 3: VOCAL COMPRESSION (3:00-5:00)

**[VISUAL: Raw vocal track, play uncompressed]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Here's a raw vocal. Notice how some words are too loud, others get lost. Let's fix that with SimpleComp."

**[VISUAL: Insert SimpleComp]**

**NARRATION**:
> "For vocals, I'll start with these settings: Threshold at minus 18 dB..."

**[VISUAL: Set Threshold to -18 dB]**

**NARRATION**:
> "...Ratio at 3:1 for gentle compression..."

**[VISUAL: Set Ratio to 3:1]**

**NARRATION**:
> "...Attack at 5 milliseconds to catch consonants..."

**[VISUAL: Set Attack to 5ms]**

**NARRATION**:
> "...and Release at 80 milliseconds for natural decay."

**[VISUAL: Set Release to 80ms]**

**[VISUAL: Play vocal with compression, show gain reduction meter hitting 4-6 dB]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Now listen - the loud parts are tamed, the quiet parts are more audible, and it sounds glued together. Watch the gain reduction meter - we're hitting about 5 dB of reduction, which is perfect for vocals."

**[VISUAL: A/B bypass comparison]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Compressed versus uncompressed - huge difference! The compressed version sits in the mix way better."

---

### PART 4: DRUM BUS COMPRESSION (5:00-7:00)

**[VISUAL: Drum bus with multiple tracks - kick, snare, hats, etc.]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Next, drum bus compression - this is how you make your drums sound like a cohesive kit instead of separate samples."

**[VISUAL: Play drums uncompressed]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Uncompressed, the drums sound fine but a bit disconnected. Let's glue them together."

**[VISUAL: Insert SimpleComp on drum bus]**

**NARRATION**:
> "For drums, I want to preserve the transients, so slower attack - 30 milliseconds."

**[VISUAL: Set Attack to 30ms]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Threshold at minus 15 dB, Ratio at 4:1 for moderate compression."

**[VISUAL: Set Threshold to -15 dB, Ratio to 4:1]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Release at 150 milliseconds - fast enough to be responsive but not pumpy."

**[VISUAL: Set Release to 150ms]**

**[VISUAL: Play drums with compression]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Hear that? The drums feel tighter and punchier. The kick and snare are more connected, and the whole kit feels like a single instrument."

**[VISUAL: Show gain reduction meter - 3-4 dB]**

**NARRATION**:
> "We're getting about 3 to 4 dB of gain reduction - just enough to glue without squashing the life out of it."

---

### PART 5: PARALLEL COMPRESSION (7:00-9:00)

**[VISUAL: Create send track with SimpleComp]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Now let's talk parallel compression - this is a pro technique where you blend heavily compressed audio with the dry signal. It gives you punch AND dynamics."

**[VISUAL: Set up aux send from drum bus to parallel compressor]**

**NARRATION**:
> "I've created a send from my drum bus to a parallel compressor track. On this parallel instance, I'm going aggressive - Threshold at minus 25 dB, Ratio at 8:1."

**[VISUAL: Set extreme compression settings]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Attack at 0.1 milliseconds to catch everything, Release at 50 milliseconds for fast response."

**[VISUAL: Solo parallel track - heavily compressed, pumping]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Listen to just the parallel track - it's squashed and pumping. Sounds terrible on its own, right? But watch what happens when I blend it with the original..."

**[VISUAL: Slowly bring up parallel send, blend with dry]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Magic! The drums have way more punch and energy, but they still feel natural because we're blending compressed with dry."

**[VISUAL: A/B with parallel on/off]**

**NARRATION**:
> "This is the secret to powerful, dynamic mixes. Parallel compression on drums, vocals, bass - game changer!"

---

### PART 6: TIPS & COMMON MISTAKES (9:00-10:30)

**[VISUAL: SimpleComp UI with labeled mistakes]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Before we wrap up, let's cover common mistakes. Mistake number one: too much compression. If you're getting more than 10 dB of gain reduction, you're probably overdoing it."

**[VISUAL: Show excessive GR (15 dB), sound pumping]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Mistake two: attack set too fast on drums. This kills the transients and makes your drums feel lifeless."

**[VISUAL: Compare fast attack (0.1ms) vs proper attack (30ms) on drums]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Hear how the slower attack preserves the punch?"

**[VISUAL: Show both waveforms side-by-side]**

**NARRATION**:
> "And mistake three: not using Auto Makeup Gain. Always enable it so you're hearing the compression effect, not just quieter audio."

**[VISUAL: Toggle Auto Makeup on/off]**

**NARRATION**:
> "Remember - compression is about control, not destruction. Start gentle and add more only if needed."
